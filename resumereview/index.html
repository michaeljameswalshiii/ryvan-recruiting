The foundation

The three questions every resume must answer.

A hiring manager should understand all three within the first few seconds.

01

What do you do?

Don’t simply list your title. Explain your primary responsibilities so someone outside your company immediately understands the role.

02

How do you do it?

Show the systems, processes, technical knowledge, leadership skills, and industry expertise that separate you from everyone else.

03

What value did you create?

Employers don’t hire responsibilities—they hire results. Include revenue, savings, time, quality, growth, safety, projects, promotions, and awards.

Make responsibility clear

Show the result

Turn responsibilities into measurable value.

Weak

Managed production operations.

Better

Managed production operations for a 120-person manufacturing facility, reducing downtime by 18%, improving on-time delivery from 89% to 97%, and implementing lean initiatives that saved over $350,000 annually.

A simple resume formula

Build stronger bullet points.

Action+Responsibility+Method+Result
Manufacturing example

Directed manufacturing operations across multiple locations by leveraging Lean Manufacturing principles to optimize production, reduce waste, and improve on-time delivery by 28%.

Accounting example

Managed accounting operations for a $12M organization while implementing automated reporting that reduced month-end close by four business days.
